Write a book, and watch it take shape.

Trame is a word processor designed for books. You write on real pages, in your book’s exact trim size; Trame lays it out like a publisher and gives you the PDF to send to the printer.

Download for Windows

Free, no account. Windows 10 and 11.

From the first word to the printed book

Trame’s five steps, in the order you’ll go through them.

Write on the real pages of your book

Text flows to the next page on its own, in the printed trim size. You always know how many pages your chapter runs to.

  • Automatic typography as you type: curly quotes, dashes, proper spacing.
  • Offline spell-checker in English, French, Spanish, Dutch, Polish and Portuguese.
  • Book outline as cards, comments, footnotes, images.
  • Import your Word, text or Markdown manuscript.

Reread like a reader

Your text appears as in a book, full screen, without the writing tools. One click, and you fix the paragraph.

  • Listen to your text read aloud, from any paragraph.
  • Spot repetitions, -ly adverbs and weak phrasing.

Publisher-grade layout, in one click

Pick a trim size and a ready-made layout; Trame typesets the book as double pages, exactly as it will come off the press.

  • 17 trim sizes, from pocket to large format, including Amazon KDP sizes.
  • 14 high-quality open fonts: Garamond, Baskerville, Caslon, Literata…
  • Drop caps, ornaments, title pages, hyphenation.

Your cover, ready for the printer

Back cover, spine and front cover on a single sheet. The spine is calculated from the page count and the paper.

  • Calculated for Amazon KDP, BoD and other printers, bleed included.
  • Templates, colour palettes, your own image, barcode area kept clear.

Three files, ready to go

Before you export, “Is my book ready?” checks what’s missing.

  • Print-ready PDF: embedded fonts, bleed, even page count.
  • EPUB ebook for Kindle, Kobo and Apple Books, with an e-reader preview.
  • Word manuscript in the format publishers and contests ask for.

Frequently asked questions

Is Trame really free?
Yes. No subscription, no ads, no account to create.
Does it work on Mac?
Not yet: Trame runs on Windows 10 and 11.
Can I publish on Amazon KDP with Trame?
Yes. Trame knows the KDP trim sizes and calculates the cover spine for KDP. You get the interior PDF and the cover PDF, ready to upload, plus an EPUB for Kindle. Before you export, “Is my book ready?” checks what’s missing.
Where are my books saved?
Only on your computer. Trame can also make an automatic backup copy in the folder of your choice (OneDrive, USB stick…), and keeps the version history of every book.
Can I continue a manuscript I’ve already started?
Yes: Trame imports Word (.docx), text and Markdown files.
Microsoft Store or direct download?
It’s the same app. From the Microsoft Store, it installs without any warning and you get the major versions. With the direct download, you get every new feature as soon as it’s out.
Why does Windows show a warning when installing?
Windows shows this message for apps from small independent developers it doesn’t know yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. From the Microsoft Store, the message doesn’t appear.
How do I report a problem or suggest an idea?
Through this form, or by message on Instagram. Every message is read.
