Install Trame on Mac

Five minutes, just once. After that, Trame updates itself.

Download for Mac

  1. Download Trame

    Click “Download for Mac”. The Trame.dmg file lands in your Downloads folder. Trame runs on recent and older Macs (Apple Silicon and Intel) with macOS 11 or later.

  2. Drag Trame into Applications

    Open Trame.dmg. A window appears: grab the Trame icon and drop it on the Applications folder, following the arrow.

  3. Open Trame

    Open the Applications folder and double-click Trame (or press ⌘ + Space and type “Trame”). The first time, your Mac says Trame couldn’t be verified: click Done, not “Move to Bin”.

  4. Allow Trame, just once

    Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the line about Trame and click Open Anyway. Confirm with your Mac password. You won’t have to do it again.

  5. You’re all set

    To keep Trame handy, drag it from Applications to the Dock. New versions then install in one click.

Opened Trame without moving it?

No problem: Trame offers to “Install in Applications”. Accept, and it moves itself there and restarts.

Why this warning?

Apple shows this message for apps that aren’t registered with it, a paid service. Trame is free and independent: it contains nothing harmful and collects no data. Your books stay on your Mac.
