What’s new

What has changed in Trame, version after version.

Full details of every version on GitHub

Version 2.6.0 Latest version

A Discord status your way

  • Settings → Discord → Customize the status: animated or still Trame logo, and you choose what your friends see (what you are doing, the title, the chapter, the words, the time spent). A preview shows the result.
  • A small icon shows the step (Write, Layout…), and a “Discover Trame” button leads to the website.
  • Layout: arrows on each side of the spread to turn pages, always visible, even when zoomed in. Thank you, Emma!

Version 2.5.1

Italics stay italic

  • Italic text inside a quote (or inside a character style that is already italic) was printed upright in Layout, the PDF and the Word manuscript. It now stays italic, just like in Write. Thank you, Emma!

Version 2.5.0

Trame 2.5: everything that’s new

  • Trame speaks English and Spanish, and your books can be written in these languages (typography, spell checker, printed words).
  • EPUB ebooks with an e-reader preview, and Word manuscripts for publishers and contests.
  • Book outline as cards, colour comments, version history and backup copy.
  • “Is my book ready?” checks everything before you publish, and the built-in help (F1) guides you.
  • And many fixes: cleaner text in Write, a top bar that fits small screens, error-free legal notice.

Version 2.1.4

Thank you!

  • About Trame: a new “Thanks” section for everyone who helps improve Trame with their ideas and feedback. Thank you, Emma!

Version 2.1.3

Read mode, made handier

  • After fixing a paragraph (the pencil), the “Back to reading” button takes you back to the same place.
  • Read: the two small buttons to the left of a paragraph (listen from here, edit) no longer vanish when you go to click them.

Version 2.1.1

Comments in colour

  • Each comment can have its own colour: choose it in the Notebook and the passage is highlighted in the same colour.
  • Layout: when you leave full screen, the settings come back by themselves. The “Reading mode” button is now called “Hide settings”.

Version 2.1.0

What's new, explained

  • After each update, this window tells you what changed. You can find it again in Settings → “What's new”.
  • Notebook: cards grow with your text, and “Enlarge” opens the card in a large view.
  • “Book outline” is easier to spot in the left column of Write.
  • Credits: spaces in names, and arrows to change the order.

Version 2.0.3

More comfort

  • Layout opens on the chapter you were writing.
  • Credits on the copyright page: proofreading, copy-editing, illustrations, cover…
  • The publisher is no longer repeated on the title page and the cover, unless you tick the box.
  • New “No indent” style (Ctrl+6), and a “None” indent setting for the whole book.

Version 2.0.0

From the first word to the published book

  • Book outline: your chapters as cards, to summarise and reorganise.
  • Comments: select a passage and click the speech bubble to leave yourself a remark.
  • Word export for publishers and contests, and EPUB ebook with an e-reader preview.
  • “Is my book ready?” checks your book before you publish (Export tab).
  • Built-in help: the “?” button or the F1 key.
